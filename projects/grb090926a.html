The evolving high-energy spectral break of GRB 090926A.

Joint GBM/LAT spectral analysis using LAT Pass 8, with constraints derived from the evolution of the high-energy spectral attenuation.

2014–2017

During my PhD at LUPM in Montpellier, I studied Fermi gamma-ray bursts through joint spectral analysis of data from the GBM and LAT instruments, including the LAT “Pass 8” data set.

My analysis of the bright GRB 090926A confirmed the previously detected spectral break at hundreds of MeV and revealed that this spectral attenuation persists at later times, with the break energy increasing into the GeV domain. Using these spectral breaks, I derived constraints on the bulk Lorentz factor of the outflow based on a model proposed by Dr. Frédéric Daigne and Dr. Robert Mochkovitch at the Institut d'Astrophysique de Paris (IAP). The results were published in A&A.
