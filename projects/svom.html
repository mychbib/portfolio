Ground calibration and instrument response

I joined the SVOM team at IRAP to work on calibration and performance activities for ECLAIRs, the wide-field coded-mask gamma-ray instrument on board the SVOM mission.

I participated in the ground-calibration phase of the ECLAIRs flight model, including studies of the detection plane and of the complete camera configuration with its coded mask.

A major part of my work focused on scientific tools used to characterize the instrument response and to generate the response files required for the analysis of ECLAIRs observations.

Instrument calibration. The objective was to characterize how the detector responds to incoming photons and to provide reliable response information for subsequent scientific spectral analysis.

Detector-response validation and scientific software

I developed analysis tools to study the homogeneity of detector responses under different instrumental configurations. In particular, I used statistical tests based on the Kolmogorov–Smirnov method to compare detector-response distributions and identify possible inconsistencies.

I also contributed to the preparation of software tools supporting Burst Advocate activities, used for the scientific follow-up and real-time monitoring of gamma-ray bursts detected by the mission.

Scientific supervision: high-redshift GRBs with ECLAIRs

During my time at IRAP, I also co-supervised a Master's research internship focused on the detection of high-redshift gamma-ray bursts with ECLAIRs. The project investigated how the instrumental response and trigger capabilities of ECLAIRs influence the detection and reconstruction of very distant GRBs.

This work initiated a research direction that was later continued and developed further during a PhD. The study evolved toward a systematic investigation of how GRBs observed at known redshifts would appear if they occurred at increasingly large distances, and how instrumental selection effects influence their measured properties.

My contribution was mainly through the initial supervision of the project, scientific discussions, follow-up of the analysis and feedback during the early stages of the work. The methodology and analysis were subsequently extended further after this initial phase.
