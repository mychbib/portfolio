CPPM · LSST DESC · HSC

Supernova-pipeline validation and image processing.

Validation of the LSST stack with simulated DESC data, supernova analysis, difference imaging and calibration work using HSC observations.

CPPM, Marseille

During my postdoctoral contract at the CPPM laboratory, I was a member of the LSST Dark Energy Science Collaboration (LSST DESC), helping validate the LSST stack pipeline with DESC simulated data. I contributed to the DC2 supernova analysis and validation of the image-processing pipeline.

This work strengthened my experience in data production and processing and introduced me to difference-image analysis (DIA). To test the pipeline on real data, I used the first public data release (PDR1) of the Hyper Suprime-Cam (HSC) instrument in the COSMOS UDEEP field in the Z and R filters. To address the large optical distortions in HSC images, I worked on improving astrometric and photometric calibration, supporting the production of accurate light curves for supernovae detected with DIA.
