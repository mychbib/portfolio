A physically motivated model for GRB MeV spectra.

Parametrising spectra produced by a detailed internal-shock model and comparing this model with commonly used descriptions of GRB spectra.

Physical modelling

Collaborators at IAP developed a detailed numerical model of relativistic jets based on the internal-shock scenario, including synchrotron radiation and inverse Compton scattering as the main radiative processes, to explain the broadband prompt emission of gamma-ray bursts.

I simulated observations of the spectra provided by the internal-shock model using the GBM and LAT responses. To parametrise the theoretical spectra, I proposed an analytical function with four parameters. I applied this physically motivated model to the analysis of a large sample of GRBs observed by GBM and also studied the sample with the Band function, commonly used to fit GRB MeV spectra.
